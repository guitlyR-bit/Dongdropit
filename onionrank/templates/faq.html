How it works

> What is OnionRANK?
A just-for-fun leaderboard of .onion domains. The ranking is determined solely by the total BTC paid.
> How do I climb higher?
Open ./boost.sh, enter the address and amount, pay in BTC, and once confirmed the amount is added.
> Can I list my site for free?
Yes – use ./submit.sh. After an admin approves it, your site appears at the bottom of the leaderboard. Any payment moves it up.
> How do I show my rank on my site?
Open your domain's page on OnionRANK and copy the badge HTML. It is a plain image that updates automatically.
> Are amounts public?
No. Only the resulting rank is shown – how much anyone paid stays private.
> Can anyone pay?
Yes – owners, fans and even competitors.
> Are payments refundable?
No. Payments are final and purely for fun.
> What about the 7/30-day boards?
They only count payments confirmed within that period.
> What about site content?
We neither verify nor endorse it. Domains with illegal content (child abuse, weapons, drugs, scams) are removed without refund.
> Do I need JavaScript?
No. Works in Tor Browser on the Safest security level.
